CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 12 June 2025 between Orion Consumer Goods Ltd ("Company") and Ms. Neha Kapoor, an independent marketing consultant ("Consultant").

1. Services. The Consultant will prepare a brand positioning study and a twelve-month digital marketing strategy for the Company's new range of organic snacks, including competitor analysis and customer interviews.

2. Fees. The Company will pay a fixed fee of INR 2,50,000 in two equal parts: on signing and on delivery of the final strategy report. Travel expenses are reimbursed at cost with prior approval.

3. Independent contractor. The Consultant is not an employee and is responsible for her own taxes. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship.

4. Ownership. The final report and presentation become the property of the Company once the fee is paid in full.

5. Term. This agreement ends on delivery of the final report or on 31 December 2025, whichever is earlier. Either party may end it with fifteen days' written notice.
