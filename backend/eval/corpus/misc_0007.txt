Grandma's mango pickle

Ingredients: 1 kg raw green mangoes, 200 g mustard oil, 4 tbsp red chilli powder, 3 tbsp mustard seeds (coarsely ground), 2 tsp fenugreek seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 100 g salt, a pinch of asafoetida.

Method: wash and fully dry the mangoes, cut into cubes with the seed shell removed. Mix with salt and turmeric and leave in the sun for a day. Heat the oil until it smokes, cool it, add the spices, then mix in the mangoes. Store in a dry glass jar and shake daily. Ready to eat in about a week.
