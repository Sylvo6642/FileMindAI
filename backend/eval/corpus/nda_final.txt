MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Theta Biotech Pvt Ltd and Iota Analytics LLP intend to discuss a possible joint research project on clinical trial data analysis. To protect information shared during these discussions, the parties agree:

1. Confidential information means any technical, commercial or scientific information disclosed by one party to the other, marked confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential.

2. The receiving party will use confidential information only to evaluate the proposed project and will not share it with third parties.

3. These obligations last for five years from the date of disclosure.

4. On request, each party will return or destroy the other party's confidential information.

This agreement does not oblige either party to enter into the joint project.
