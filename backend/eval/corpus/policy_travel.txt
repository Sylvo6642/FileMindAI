Employee Travel and Expense Policy

Scope: all employees travelling on company business.

Air travel: economy class for flights under six hours; book at least fourteen days in advance where possible.

Hotels: up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere.

Daily allowance: INR 1,500 per day for meals and local transport.

Claims: submit expense claims with original receipts within thirty days of returning. Claims are approved by the reporting manager and reimbursed with the next salary.
