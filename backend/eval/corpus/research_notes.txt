Reading notes - evaluating machine learning models

Precision measures how many predicted positives are correct; recall measures how many actual positives were found. For imbalanced data, accuracy alone is misleading.

Cross-validation: split the data into k folds, train on k-1 and test on the remaining fold, repeat and average.

Overfitting shows up as a gap between training and validation scores. Remedies: more data, regularisation, simpler models, early stopping.
